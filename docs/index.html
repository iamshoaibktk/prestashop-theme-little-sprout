Questions from parents

Read the size guide
How do I choose the right size?expand_more

Our sizes follow age, but height is more reliable. Check the size guide, and if your child is between sizes, take the larger one.

Are your clothes safe for babies?expand_more

Yes. All fabrics are tested for harmful substances, we use nickel-free poppers and there are no drawstrings at the neck.

How should I wash organic cotton?expand_more

Wash at 30–40 °C with similar colours and avoid the tumble dryer to keep the fabric soft and the fit right.

Can I exchange a size?expand_more

Of course. Return the item within 30 days and place a new order for the right size.
